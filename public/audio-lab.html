Audio lab

Live audio and slide watching, without the rest of the app

Source

Three ways to feed the engine. Capture a tab: pick a tab with talking in it (the Zoom web client or a YouTube lecture) and turn on "Also share tab audio"; that tab goes quiet and this page plays it instead. Simulated meeting: a made-up voice with short and long pauses, and a made-up shared screen with slides and a moving camera tile; nothing to set up. Audio file: the same simulated screen, with a recording of your choice as the voice.

Nothing captured.

Controls

Shortcuts: H hold, U catch up, L skip to live, T run the 30-second test, N next simulated slide, Q make the simulated presenter go quiet.
